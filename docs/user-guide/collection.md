# Managing your collection

## Signing in

Click **Sign in** in the top right. New collectors can register from the sign-in page. Your collection is private: no other collector can see or find your cards.

## Adding a card

Go to **Collection → Add card** and fill in two sections.

**Card** describes what the card is:
- **Sport, Year, Brand, Set, Card #, Card name** are required. Set and player fields suggest names Card Maven already knows, so pick a suggestion when it matches. That keeps everyone's copies of the same card linked, which matters once market prices arrive.
- **Players:** separate multiple players with commas (for dual or triple cards).
- **Subset:** the insert or subset within the product, like *Chrome Prospect Autographs*. It matters because the same parallel name (say *Refractor /499*) can have different print runs in different subsets.
- **Parallel and Print run:** leave Parallel blank for a base card. For a numbered parallel, the print run is the denominator: `/50` → `50`.
- **Rookie, Autograph, Memorabilia:** tick whichever apply.

**Your copy** describes the card in your hands:
- **Condition:** Raw or Graded. Graded cards need a grader (PSA, BGS, SGC, CGC, TAG) and a grade from 1 to 10 in half-point steps. The cert number is optional.
- **Serial #:** the numerator on numbered cards (`12/50` → `12`). It can't exceed the print run.
- **Price paid, Acquired, Notes:** for your own records.

## Browsing

The collection page shows cards as a **Grid** (with front photos) or a **Table** (with prices). Search matches player, card name, set, team, parallel, and card number. Filter by sport, year, or condition, and sort by recently added, year, or name. Filters are part of the page address, so you can bookmark a view. **Load more** fetches the next page.

**Est. value** is the card's market estimate (see [Comps and estimated values](market.md)). It shows "—" until there are recent sales at your card's grade.

## Photos

Open a card and use **Upload** under Front or Back. JPEG, PNG, WebP, and HEIC photos up to 15 MB are accepted. **Replace** swaps the photo, and **Remove** deletes it. (HEIC photos upload fine but may not display in Chrome or Firefox yet. iPhones usually convert to JPEG when uploading from Safari.)

## Editing and deleting

Open a card and choose **Edit** to change anything, including which card it is. **Delete** removes the card and its photos after you confirm. This can't be undone.
